MarketMind Lab: six logo directions

Six ways the logo could go, each clearly different from the others. Every direction is shown large, as an app icon and favicon in three sizes, on a light and on a dark ground, and in the header of the app. All of them use the colours and the two typefaces of the style guide.

Nothing in the app has changed: the logo in use today is below for comparison.

MarketMind Lab

Today. The letter M drawn as a line with three points, in a rounded square, next to the name in the serif of the headlines.

1

Wordmark

The name alone, set in the serif of the headlines, with “Mind” in italics: the quietest option, and the one closest to a university press.

2

Monogram

Two letters M, one standing on the reflection of the other, in a round seal: a result and the thinking behind it.

3

Mind and data

A thought bubble that holds a rising bar chart: the tool does not give the answer, it makes the student think about the numbers.

4

Perceptual map

Two axes and the three marks of the app’s segments (circle, square, triangle): the picture a marketing analyst knows best.

5

Lab flask

A laboratory flask whose liquid ends in a rising line: “Lab” taken literally, a place to try things out on data.

6

Market meets mind

Two overlapping circles with the overlap filled in: the market and the mind, and the product as the place where they meet.

These are directions, not finished artwork. Each mark is drawn on a grid of 48 by 48 units and stays readable at 16 pixels. For a final logo the chosen mark would be refined, the name would be turned from type into outlines, and the files would be exported as SVG and PNG in the usual sizes. The source of every mark is in this file, in the block of symbols at its top.
